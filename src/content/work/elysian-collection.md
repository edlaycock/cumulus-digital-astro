challenge: Elysian Collection offers bespoke sales and marketing representation for a hand-picked portfolio of luxury hotels and resorts across the UK and Ireland. It needed a website as considered as the properties it represents — editorial, calm and unmistakably premium — speaking to hoteliers weighing up representation and to the travel trade alike.
approach: This sat squarely in our travel-industry wheelhouse. We designed and built a quietly luxurious site led by full-bleed photography and restrained serif typography, with clear routes for hotels to enquire about representation and for partners to explore the collection — the calm, high-end feel a luxury brand needs to be taken seriously.
result: A polished, editorial presence that reflects the calibre of the hotels Elysian represents, with confident enquiry paths for prospective hotel partners throughout.
