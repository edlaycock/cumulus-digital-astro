challenge: Formerly Gallery One in Reigate, the gallery relocated to Tetbury and rebranded as The Gallery Tetbury. It sells vibrant original paintings and sculpture direct from artists' studios, chosen on quality rather than reputation, and needed a clean, welcoming website that let the art itself take centre stage under the new name.
approach: A fairly minimalist website that shows off the artists, paintings and sculpture the gallery represents — carried across from the original Reigate site and refreshed for the move to Tetbury.
result: The Gallery Tetbury has an uncluttered, quality-led website that showcases its artists' work and reflects its friendly, approachable style.
