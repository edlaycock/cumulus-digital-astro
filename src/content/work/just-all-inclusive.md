challenge: The brand began life as JA Resorts Holidays, selling breaks at award-winning beach and mountain resorts across the UAE and Indian Ocean, and had to convey five-star escapism the moment a visitor landed. The site needed to sell the feeling of a luxury break, sun, sea and space, while making it easy to move from inspiration to booking.
approach: We led with full-bleed resort photography, infinity pools, palms and beachfront, so the destinations carry the page. Cool blues and warm stone tones drawn from the pool-and-sand imagery keep the palette calm and premium, with the wordmark kept understated over the visuals so nothing competes with the setting.
result: A polished, image-first experience that matches the upmarket positioning of the resorts and invites travellers to picture their stay. The brand has since moved to its current name, Just All Inclusive, and the original jaresortsholidays.com address now forwards there.
