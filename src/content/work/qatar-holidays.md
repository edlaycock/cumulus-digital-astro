challenge: Qatar Holidays sells luxury breaks to Doha and beyond to UK travellers, competing with the big online travel agents on some of the most famous hotel names in the Gulf. The site had to sell the destination, make twelve five-star hotels easy to compare, and turn interest into a phone call or a booking.
approach: We built a destination-first site that leads with a full-width Doha skyline slider and a clear low-deposit offer, followed by a hotel carousel showing the current discount on each property. Every hotel, from Raffles Doha and the Four Seasons to Marsa Malaz Kempinski and Banana Island Resort, has its own page, alongside sections for the destination itself, special offers and holiday types. The phone number and Book Now button stay in view on every screen, desktop and mobile.
result: A polished, conversion-focused destination site that presents Qatar's luxury hotels and live offers to UK travellers, with clear booking paths throughout. It sits alongside our Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah builds as part of a family of Gulf destination-specialist sites.
