Foundations

Colour

A white canvas throughout, black display type, and one signal colour. Brand navy is the working accent: it clears contrast on white, so it carries CTAs, links and accent words. Brand teal is artwork, tints and dark grounds only.

Signal

Surfaces

Ink

Depth comes from hairline borders and large radii, never from drop shadows.

Foundations

Type

Satoshi sets everything: headlines, reading copy and labels. Instrument Serif italic appears only on the highlighted words in a heading, never for body copy or a whole heading.
